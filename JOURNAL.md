# Journal

The project diary. Append-only, newest at the bottom, a short section per session. The agent
keeps it; `/status` reads it. See [`base/BOOKKEEPING.md`](base/BOOKKEEPING.md) for the
contract, [`base/SAFETY.md`](base/SAFETY.md) for the Green/Yellow/Red model.

Start your project's history here — replace the example entry below with your own. Each
entry records one working session: what changed, how you verified it, and what's left.

---

## YYYY-MM-DD — Example entry (replace me)

A session entry looks like this: short, past tense, concrete. Say what you
built or changed, how you verified it (a green build, the tests, a manual check), and
anything left open for next time. Keep it honest — a journal that also notes what broke,
and why, is more useful later than one that records only successes.
