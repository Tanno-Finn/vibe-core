# Participant information and consent

You are taking part in a small pilot of a tool for lesson preparation. This page tells you
what it is about, what happens to the information you provide, and what you can do at any
time. Please read it before you sign.

## What this is about

The pilot tests an openly available tool package with which an AI coding agent drafts
teaching material — worksheets, differentiated tasks, cover lessons. We want to find out
whether what it produces is usable in your real preparation, and where it is not. You operate
the tool yourself, on a topic you bring along. There is nothing to pass; an "I wouldn't use
this" is a full-fledged result.

The session lasts 60 to 90 minutes. No compensation is paid.

## No student data

Please do **not enter any student data** and do not bring any along: no names, no grades, no
learning-support or health notes, no class lists. The tool is built to stop when it receives
input like that — the pilot tests this as well, but the rule applies regardless. For tasks
that concern children, please use made-up names or labels such as "Child A".

## What we record

- Two short questionnaires (before and after) that you fill in yourself.
- An observation sheet on which a second person notes what happens during the work: times,
  the number of follow-up questions, points where you pause, and sentences you say out loud.
- The materials the tool produced during the session.

All records carry only a **code** (L1, L2, L3). Only the person running the pilot knows which
code belongs to which name; this key is not stored with the sheets and is destroyed after the
evaluation. School, location, and subject are named in the evaluation only so broadly that no
one can infer who you are.

## No recording without your consent

There is **no** filming and **no** audio recording unless you explicitly check the box for it
below. Screenshots of the generated materials contain no information about you.

## What the results are used for

The tool package is open source. The summarized results of the pilot — findings, requests for
changes, anonymized quotes — feed into its further development and may appear in the package's
public documentation, in the form: "In a pilot with three teachers …". Your name appears
nowhere. A verbatim quote is published only if it does not allow anyone to infer who you are;
when in doubt, it is reworded or left out.

The generated materials contain your lesson topic, but no information about you or your
school. They are only evaluated, not published — unless you separately agree to that below.

## Your rights

- You can **stop taking part at any time**, without giving reasons.
- You can **withdraw your consent until the evaluation is published**; your sheets and
  materials are then destroyed and not evaluated. After that, withdrawal is no longer possible
  for anonymized summaries that have already been published, because they can no longer be
  traced back to you.
- You can find out at any time what has been recorded under your code.

This information is not legal advice. If your school or your employer (in Germany, the
*Dienstherr*) requires approval for taking part in a pilot like this, please obtain it
beforehand — the person running the pilot will support you with this description.
