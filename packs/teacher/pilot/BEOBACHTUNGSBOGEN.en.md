# Observation sheet — one sheet per teacher

The observer keeps this sheet during the work phases. The observer does not step in, explains
nothing, judges nothing. They write down **what happens** — times, numbers, exact wording.
Interpretation comes later, in the evaluation.

**Teacher's code:** ______   **Session (date):** ______________   **Observer:** ______

**Topic the teacher brought along:** ______________________________________

---

## Per job

For every job that this teacher **operated themselves**. The clock starts when the teacher has
heard the task and stops at the first result that they **call usable** (not at the first
result of any kind). If they never say so, the field stays empty and the "Result" field
carries the word *none*.
